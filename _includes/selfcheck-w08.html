Four questions from Week 8 learning objectives, the Study checklist, and named formulas (Sdelay / MCC, Bmax, Sday). Capacity is a whole-system concept; Sandbox ≠ Sandbox X; U-space ≠ HK law.

1. Safe operating capacity in Week 8 is…
Learning objective 1; Study checklist 1–3. Safe capacity depends on the whole operating system and its binding resource for a defined time. Sandbox X trials do not publish a general Harbour corridor capacity; the ~1,000 m policy description is not a Cap. 448G flying limit or a capacity figure.
2. Marginal congestion cost (MCC) in the classroom sketch is…
Learning objective 3; Study checklist 4; Formulas · MCC. Value the incremental delay and other costs imposed on others, not the operator's own contribution, historical delay, or slot bid. The result is a classroom allocation aid, not a published Hong Kong tariff.
3. An operator’s maximum affordable slot payment Bmax is…
Study checklist 6–7; Formulas · Bmax. Bmax = RΔ − CΔ − πreq. It is the operator's private limit, not proof of social priority. A flight lacking linked departure, corridor, arrival, or charging slots must be rejected or rescheduled, never flown unsafely.
4. Which digital / programme statement matches Week 8?
Learning objective 4–5; Study checklist 8–12; Formulas · Sday. Illustrative interoperability savings subtract integrated coordination cost from legacy cost; test whether service quality and security hold. OFCA's Sandbox-confined spectrum note and AC-017's Sandbox X cross-boundary scope are separate. U-space is not Hong Kong law.
