Four questions from Week 3 learning objectives, the Study checklist, and named formulas (CAPEX/OPEX, CASK, breakeven). Keep the Hong Kong premium as hypotheses to test; Sandbox ≠ Sandbox X.

1. On the Week 3 cost stack, the first discipline is to…
Learning objective 1; Study checklist 1–2, 4; §§2–3. Hong Kong premium is a hypothesis, not a universal multiplier. CASK is cost per available seat-km offered — not proof of profit (Formulas · CASK).
2. CASK (cost per available seat-kilometre) is…
Study checklist 5–6; §4; Formulas · CASK. Mission-km is a different unit for cargo / public service. CASK does not by itself prove commercial viability.
3. Breakeven units in the Week 3 classroom identity are…
Learning objective 3; Study checklist 8–9; §7; Formulas · Breakeven. The denominator is contribution per unit: revenue minus variable cost. After calculating required units, check whether capacity and demand can support them.
4. Which revenue-model statement matches Week 3?
Learning objective 4–5; Study checklist 7, 11–12; §§5, 8. Availability payments buy readiness and usually carry service, refund, or credit obligations. Subscriptions depend on accepted terms and continued payment; data services need rights and customers, not just data.
