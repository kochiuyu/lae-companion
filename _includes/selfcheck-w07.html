Five questions from Week 7 learning objectives, the Study checklist, and named formulas (readiness / compliance appraisal). Cap. 448G risk-based SUA facts; Sandbox ≠ Sandbox X; AC-013 ≠ AC-017; U-space ≠ HK law.

1. Cap. 448G in these notes is framed as…
Learning objective 1; Study checklist 3; Week 7 policy sections. Cap. 448G is the Hong Kong risk-based SUA frame; advanced operations need prior CAD permission. It is not a vertiport code or a commercial franchise. U-space is an EU comparator, not Hong Kong law (Study checklist 9).
2. Which pair correctly separates sandbox instruments?
Study checklist 4–5; Learning objective 1–2. AC-013's BVLOS pathway sits within the first Regulatory Sandbox; AC-017 addresses Sandbox X cross-boundary procedures. The programmes and circulars are distinct. Neither circular grants BVLOS everywhere or a permanent commercial route right.
3. Net readiness value (classroom) is…
Study checklist 1–2; Formulas · net readiness value. A shorter useful lead can erase readiness value. Approval probability belongs to the separate compliance appraisal; avoided redesign cost belongs to trial value. Use contribution, not gross ticket revenue.
4. A safety case in Week 7 is for…
Learning objective 3; Study checklist 6, 8. A safety case addresses hazards and controls for a defined operation; it does not replace the separate permission, operator/pilot, aircraft, and site gates. Risk cost includes prevention, insurance, and retained expected loss. A trial corridor does not prove routine service (Study checklist 12).
5. Digital traffic-management / coordination in Week 7…
Learning objective 4–5; Study checklist 9–11; Formulas · daily coordination cost. Digital coordination can help scale, but oversight, provider, and cybersecurity costs remain. U-space is a comparator, not Hong Kong law; a selected UTM trial is not proof of city-wide daily service.
