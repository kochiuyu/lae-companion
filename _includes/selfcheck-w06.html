Four questions from Week 6 learning objectives, the Study checklist, and named formulas (monthly economic profit, RU / RA, shared facility cost). A PPP does not invent demand, statute, or permissions. Sandbox ≠ Sandbox X.

1. Asset-intensive, service-based, and platform-oriented models are compared in Week 6 mainly on…
Learning objective 1; Study checklist 1–3; §§2–3. Asset-light does not erase performance or liability risk, and revenue share alone cannot show who controls assets or depends on partners. Compare the architectures alongside the monthly economic-profit screen: Formulas · Week 6.
2. A public–private partnership (PPP) value-for-money test…
Learning objective 2; Study checklist 4; § PPP discussion. Payment mechanisms and KPIs still align quality, affordability, and viability (Learning objective 3).
3. In the availability-based component RA = A − D + u × q…
Study checklist 5–6; Formulas · RA. A is the base availability payment, D the deductions, u a per-unit usage payment, and q the delivered units. D can reduce revenue when targets are missed; RA is revenue, not profit. Usage-based RU = p × q is the companion identity.
4. Shared-pad co-opetition in Week 6 means…
Learning objective 4–5; Study checklist 8–12; §§ shared pads / term sheet. Sharing a facility need not remove competition between operators. Facility cost per movement is not the full operator ticket cost; a term sheet should allocate access, risk, KPIs, and relief. Sandbox participation grants no permanent commercial slot right.
